PSX SHADER KIT

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open project.godot with Godot 4.7.2 (Compatibility).
3. Press F5. Press Esc for settings; hold Tab to compare.

To use only the shaders, copy PSXRender into your own Godot project.
Follow PSXRender/README.md for scene setup and the psx_wobble group.

This download is editable Godot source, not a browser game or Windows executable.
Web/mobile are not tested. See LICENSE.txt for usage terms.
